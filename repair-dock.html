    Repair guide and options

    This dock is safe. Repair attempts do not use campaign hull, score, parts, or equipment.

    Left / Right: steer. Space: fire manually. Left Shift: fold. Right Shift: unfold. C: capture. E: release. P: pause or resume.

    Distance circuit: charge fades after 1.2 seconds. Compress the route to the left receiver.

    Return circuit: the dock launcher points up and right. Fire from the right mark. Its signal crosses the real Klein side join.

    Echo circuit: catch a filled charge near the right mark. Move to the left receiver. Release the stored echo.

    The receiver ring and the text report each successful delivery. The earlier cause remains below the field.
