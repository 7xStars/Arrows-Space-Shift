# 240 BPM stereo comparison

The analysis filters actual PCM files and measures each 250 ms window.
The selected comparison interval is 20 through 44 seconds.

| Signal | Baseline upper-band balance deviation | Revised upper-band balance deviation |
|---|---:|---:|
| full | 0.1789 | 0.2242 |
| core | 0.1796 | 0.2244 |
| atmosphere | 0.1435 | 0.6378 |

Kick and low-bass source routes remain centered. The 120 Hz crossover is not a brick-wall filter. Existing delays, residual upper-bass content, and ambience can produce low-band channel differences. These measurements do not claim a perfectly mono complete low band or human recognition.

These results do not establish musical approval. The supplied WAVs permit a separate listening comparison.
