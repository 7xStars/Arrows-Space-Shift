Only the arrow keys control the ship. Only Left Shift and Right Shift change space. Hold Space to fire.

What changes?

A fold halves distance inside one field. It also doubles the visible speed of both sides’ shots. Overlapping folds multiply these effects.

The clock keeps its normal rate. Unfolding does not reverse the flight.
