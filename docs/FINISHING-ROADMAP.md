## Non-negotiable design constraints

- Preserve Act I's traditional-scrolling-shmup stages and Klein Fleet difficulty/controls.
- Act II develops authored bullet hell on the actual Klein traversal surface. Its hybrid K#RP² boss awards the Projective Module; the prototype quotient is not a proven connected sum.
- Act III develops player agency and bullet-heaven escalation via deliberate capture/release, retained manual fire, and the Projective Module. The Curvature Gate awards a Curvature Seed, **not** a finished Hyperbolic Drive.
- Eight teaching stages and one ninth-stage culminating geometry encounter, with calm spaces after lessons.
- Preserve the lower-half viewport movement and keep reverse thrust bounded at the bottom. Keep the scroll moving forward.
- New power must change available actions, not just multiply damage. Ship roles retain strengths and weaknesses.
- Bank a unique boss part only after collection and extraction. Maintain additive save migration.
