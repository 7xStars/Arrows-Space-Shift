# Arrow Space Shift

Change space instead of changing thrust.

Fold the route to make shots arrive sooner.
Enemy shots gain speed too.
Unfold to restore distance and get more reaction time.

Learn to steer and fire in Level 0.
Then cross eight routes and face the Klein Fleet.
Even-numbered routes have mostly asteroids and occasional enemies.
Both sides can break asteroids with their shots.
Enemies that leave the bottom return at the top with their remaining hull.
Stage 7 uses faster carriers and Klein bottle edge paths.

## Controls

Use a keyboard.
Select the game view before you play.

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| Steer left or right | Left / Right arrow |
| Fire | Hold Space |
| Fold space | Left Shift |
| Unfold the latest field | Right Shift |
| Pause or resume | P or Escape |
| Change equipment | Equipment button |
| Retry or start a new run | Options button |

Up and Down have no action.
The route controls forward travel.
Each Shift press makes one change.
You can keep three folds active.
Folding and unfolding can be repeated throughout the run.

A right Position Warp seed accepts right-side shots with one or three folds.
With zero or two folds, it accepts left-side shots.
A left seed has the opposite rule.
The current accepted side appears in the game view.

Equipment and Guide pause play.
Closing a panel leaves play paused.
Select Resume flight to continue.
Retry restores the current section checkpoint.
New run clears the campaign and creates a new layout.
Progress lasts until the page is closed or reloaded.

The final battle joins opposite edges of the map.
Crossing a side edge reverses vertical position and facing.
Enemies retain their health when they cross an edge.
Destroy the core and both escorts to finish.
