## itch.io description

A damaged Warp Drive. Three Acts. Change the space around each threat.

Arrow Space Shift is a manual-fire shoot-'em-up about changing the space around a threat.
Fold distance to accelerate a shot. The incoming fire accelerates too.
Read returning attacks on a Klein surface.
Capture a hostile projectile and decide when to release its echo.

Each Act changes how movement and attacks relate to the field.
The black starfield stays quiet so you can read the live threats and future images.
Solid projectiles can collide. Hollow images forecast a return; they are not extra projectiles.

Defeat the bosses, collect their physical drive parts, and extract them.
Finish Act III by extracting the Curvature Seed from the final encounter.
Replay reached sections with your saved ships and equipment.
Section checkpoints let you leave and return.

This downloadable development candidate has automated and local browser verification.
Human balance, listening, and physical-device acceptance remain in progress.

## Controls

| Mode | Controls |
|---|---|
| Act I | Left / Right steer; hold Space to fire; Left Shift folds; Right Shift unfolds |
| Acts II–III | Left / Right steer; Space fires; Left Shift folds; Right Shift unfolds; C captures and E releases where unlocked |
| Pause | P; Options and Guide pause play |

Keyboard is the primary tested input.
Sound, independent mix levels, reduced motion, and decorative effects save in this browser.
Critical warnings remain visual when sound is off.
Browser storage is origin-specific. Use full progress export before changing origin or browser.
