Controls

Use ←/→ (A/D) to turn. Use ↑/↓ (W/S) for thrust. Hold Space for manual fire. C captures nearby hostile shots. E releases stored echoes. Act IV stores up to six echoes. Shift starts a short fold phase. It increases both friendly and hostile projectile speed. P pauses or resumes play. On touch, hold a direction button and Fire.

Read the surface

A–D connect to A′–D′. Solid projectiles can cause damage after a crossing. A hollow dashed marker shows a future position. It has no separate hitbox. The outer unit circle is not a game boundary.

Development limits

Act IV v0.25.1-dev.5 uses a separate renderer and the existing Poincaré-disk Bolza kernel. The Curvature Seed unlocks this Act. A separate stage checkpoint saves its progress. Collision checks use a depth-one quotient neighborhood and bounded simulation steps. Global continuous collision, dense combat performance, and human play remain unverified. The extracted core opens the repair dock. Equipment operation effects remain inactive in this chart.
