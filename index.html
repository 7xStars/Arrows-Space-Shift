Section guide and campaign route

Damaged Warp Drive

The damaged drive sometimes folds space on its own. An amber warning appears before the surge. Right Shift removes the latest fold. Left Shift can still add a fold. The clock and firing rate keep their normal pace.

Read the firing pattern

Level 7 introduces FAN, SWEEP, and GAP. FAN commits a spread. SWEEP sends a timed sequence across several directions. GAP leaves an opening in its launch pattern. Read the charge and launch marks, then steer. Other shots can still cross the opening.

The Klein Fleet surface

The Klein Fleet demonstrates the edge joins before you can inhabit them. After recovering the Klein Relay, the ship traverses that same twisted surface in Act II: a side crossing reflects the longitudinal position and reverses physical forward direction. The camera carries the viewing frame so the ship still faces up on screen and remains in the lower half of the arena. The level keeps scrolling forward. Left and Right steer; hold Space to fire forward.

Ships, shots, and seeds return through the joined edges. A side join reverses the surface's vertical direction. Top and bottom join without that twist. Line up with dropped seeds as they scroll toward you. The fleet keeps its damage when it crosses an edge.

Recover the relay

Destroy the core and both escorts. Fly into the Klein Relay to collect it, then cross the exit line. Defeating the fleet does not collect the part for you. Select a ship, then launch into Act II.

Act II · Klein bullet hell

Eight stages keep the same Fan, Sweep, Gap, Aimed, Wall and Return grammar while teaching surface travel, intentional seam crossing, returning fire, global gaps, pattern layering, fold timing, density, and Klein mastery. Each lesson is followed by a recovery interval.

Projective Intrusion

The ninth encounter combines the learned Klein side joins with projective top/bottom identification. Watch the geometry mark on each warning. Destroy the intrusion, physically collect the Projective Module, and extract it. The module is saved beside the Klein Relay.

Act III · Projective Ecology

Eight stages teach projective chart travel, capture (C), release (E), ordered synthesis, harvesting, pressure, powered agency, and mastery. Each stage contains quiet recovery and up to two Echo Forge choices. The gun remains manually controlled with Space. The projective chart is an experimental edge-identification model, not a verified RP² metric or surface implementation.

Curvature Gate

The ninth boss previews the next geometry. The Gate is an experimental projectile encounter, not the finished hyperbolic double torus. Collect its Curvature Seed and extract.
